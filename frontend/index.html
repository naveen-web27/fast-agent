RightConnect on your phone

Your next connection, a tap away.

Use RightConnect in your browser or download our Android app directly from this website.

Download Android APK Continue on the web

Android only. Installation is optional. This is a direct APK download, not a Google Play link.

Install on Android

  1. Download the APK on your Android phone.
  2. Open the downloaded file. If Android asks, allow your browser to install this app only if you trust this website.
  3. Finish installation and open RightConnect. You can turn off your browser's install permission afterwards.
Using an iPhone or iPad?

The APK cannot be installed on iOS. Open RightConnect in Safari, tap Share (or More, then Share), choose Add to Home Screen, and tap Add. If you are in another app's browser, open this website in Safari first.

Prefer not to install anything?

All visitors can continue to the marketplace in their browser. Installing the app is never required to browse or create an account.
