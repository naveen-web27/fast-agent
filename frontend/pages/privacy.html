Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 6, 2026

This policy explains how RightConnect handles information when you visit rightconnect.app, create an account, use the marketplace, contact a provider, or use provider plans. RightConnect is a marketplace operated under the name RightConnect. For privacy questions, contact rightconnect.co@gmail.com.

Information we collect

  • Account and sign-in information: Google sign-in provides account identifiers and may provide your name, email address, and profile image. We use Supabase Auth to manage sign-in.
  • Information you provide: name, email, optional phone number, city, preferred language, interests, role, biography, service and experience details, credentials, company information, website and social links, availability, and profile image URL.
  • Marketplace and communications: service requests, request status, messages sent in a request workspace, appointment details, ratings, reviews, and information you choose to include in a public profile.
  • Plan and transaction records: selected plan, amount, payment status, payment-provider identifiers, and timestamps. Razorpay processes the checkout. RightConnect does not receive or store full card numbers or card security codes through the checkout integration.
  • Technical information: information needed to deliver and secure the site, such as request and error logs processed by our hosting and infrastructure providers. Your browser also connects to services used for Google sign-in, Supabase, Razorpay checkout, and web fonts.

How we use information

  • Create and secure your account and let you use the role and identity you selected.
  • Show provider profiles, help customers find and compare services, and deliver requests, messages, appointments, and reviews.
  • Display public profile information you choose to publish. Contact details are revealed in a request only under the product's participant acceptance rules.
  • Process provider-plan checkout, verify payment with Razorpay, and apply the purchased plan to the correct expert profile or company.
  • Send company-domain verification emails when requested, provide customer support, maintain security, prevent abuse, and operate the service.

Information visibility and sharing

Information in a public expert or company profile, such as its display name, description, services, experience, credentials, links, ratings, and reviews, may be visible to visitors. Request messages and details are available to the participants in that request. Contact information is shared only as described above or when you choose to publish it yourself.

We use service providers to operate RightConnect: Supabase for authentication and database services; Render for application hosting; Google for sign-in; Razorpay for payment checkout and payment processing; Resend for requested company verification emails; and third-party content delivery/font services used by the website. These providers process information needed to deliver their services under their own terms and privacy practices. We do not sell personal information. We may disclose information where required by law or to protect users, the service, or legal rights.

Payments

Provider plans are optional, one-time 30-day purchases. Razorpay hosts the payment interface and processes payment details. RightConnect receives payment and order identifiers, amount, status, and related transaction details to confirm the purchase and provide the plan. Payment data is also subject to Razorpay's privacy policy.

Storage, security, and retention

RightConnect and its service providers process information on systems located in the regions configured for the service. Data is transmitted over HTTPS where supported. We use access controls intended to limit access to operational needs. No method of transmission or storage can be guaranteed completely secure.

We retain account and profile information while an account is active and retain request, review, and transaction records as needed to operate the marketplace, resolve issues, maintain security, and meet legal obligations. Retention periods vary by record type and applicable requirements. Razorpay may separately retain payment information under its policies and legal obligations.

Account and data deletion

You can initiate account deletion from the account settings in RightConnect, or contact RightConnect support to request account deletion. Include the email used for the account; never email a password, one-time code, or payment card details. We may need to verify that you control the account before acting.

Important: The current in-app deletion action marks account, profile, and some company records as deleted and hides them from normal use. These records may remain in the database and may be restorable; a permanent deletion schedule has not yet been implemented. Contact support to discuss a deletion request and any records that must be retained. This limitation must be resolved before RightConnect claims permanent account/data deletion in a store declaration.

Children

RightConnect is designed for customers and professional service providers, not for children. The service is not intended for people under 18, and we do not knowingly seek to collect children's information. Contact us if you believe a child has provided information.

Choices and updates

You can edit some profile information in the service, choose what information to include in a public profile, and stop using the service. We may update this policy as the service or legal requirements change. The effective date above shows when this version took effect. Material changes will be reflected on this page.
