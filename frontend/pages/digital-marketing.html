Questions

Good to know.

Do I need a website to run ads?

No. Your ads can open a WhatsApp chat with you directly, so customers can message you in one tap.

Where do the ads run?

Mainly Facebook and Instagram, where most local customers spend their time. Ask us if you want something else and we'll tell you honestly whether it fits your business.

How much does it cost?

It depends on how many posters you need and how much you want to spend on ads. Message us with your business and goal and we'll share a clear plan and price before anything starts.

What do you need from me?

Your logo (if you have one), a few photos of your shop, product or work, the offer you want to promote and your area. We'll handle the rest.

How will I know if the ads are working?

Every enquiry from your ads is tracked: who asked, whether they were called, and whether they became a customer. You get a simple summary so the next campaign can do better.
