# Independent experts: English video ad

Audience: Independent professionals, consultants, and advisors offering legitimate services.

Goal: Create an account and complete an expert profile. Existing customers can add an expert identity through the workspace menu.

Tone: Respectful and practical. Emphasize explaining expertise, not guaranteed customer acquisition.

Production/claims rules: [team brief](README.md).

## Main concept: "Give your expertise a clear introduction"

> Help potential customers understand your expertise. Show your services and experience with a RightConnect expert profile. Give people a clear introduction to your work. Get started at rightconnect.app.

**Edit:** 0-3s hook; 3-10s profile demo; 10-15s CTA.
