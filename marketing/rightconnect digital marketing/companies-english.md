# Companies: English video ad

Audience: Service-business owners and authorized company representatives.

Goal: Create a company profile and clearly describe the company's services.

Tone: Practical and confident, focused on clarity and useful conversations rather than promised sales.

Production/claims rules: [team brief](README.md).

## Main concept: "Give customers a clearer picture of your company"

### 30-second storyboard

| Target time | Visual direction | Voiceover | On-screen text |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 0-5s | Small service-business owner preparing an introduction for a potential customer. | Can potential customers quickly understand what your company offers? | Make your services easier to understand. |
| 5-10s | Original logo into actual company setup/profile footage. | Create your company profile on RightConnect. | RightConnect / Company profile |
| 10-18s | Company description and service details, shown using the real interface. | Introduce your business and put your services and company details in one place. | Your company. Your services. |
| 18-24s | Authorized representative reviewing a demonstration enquiry in the real workspace. | Review incoming enquiries, respond to customer needs, and take the conversation forward. | From an enquiry to a conversation. |
| 24-30s | Clean branded end card with readable destination. | Visit rightconnect.app and start building your company profile. | Create company profile / rightconnect.app |

## 15-second cut

> Give potential customers a clearer picture of your business. Create a RightConnect company profile, introduce your services, and respond to incoming enquiries. Get started at rightconnect.app.

**Edit:** 0-3s hook; 3-10s company profile footage; 10-15s CTA.

## Alternative hooks

1. **Explain your services:** "Your company does good work. Make it easier for people to understand what you offer."
2. **Before an enquiry:** "Give potential customers a place to get to know your company before they reach out."
3. **One introduction:** "Your services, your company story, and the next conversation. Start with a clear profile."
