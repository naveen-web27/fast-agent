# Customers: English video ad

Audience: Adults looking for professional help for themselves, their family, or their business.

Goal: Open the marketplace, inspect relevant profiles, and send an introduction request.

Tone: Friendly Indian English. Helpful, clear, and grounded, not a promise of guaranteed results.

Production/claims rules: [team brief](README.md).

## Main concept: "You know what you need. But whom should you approach?"

### 30-second storyboard

| Target time | Visual direction | Voiceover | On-screen text |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 0-5s | Adult customer or small-business owner comparing search results, unsure where to begin. | Need help with your business, but not sure whom to approach? | Need help. Not more guesswork. |
| 5-10s | Original logo into an actual marketplace recording. | Meet RightConnect. Explore experts and companies in one place. | RightConnect / Experts + Companies |
| 10-17s | Search for a live category; open relevant profiles and service/experience details. | Look through their services, experience, and profile details to find a suitable fit. | Explore. Compare details. Choose. |
| 17-24s | Show request submission, then a clearly separate accepted-request workspace. | Send a request. Once the provider accepts, start discussing what you need. | Request → Acceptance → Conversation |
| 24-30s | Clean navy end card, logo and URL. | Visit rightconnect.app and explore the marketplace today. | Explore the marketplace / rightconnect.app |

## 15-second cut

> Need a service, but not sure whom to contact? Explore experts and companies on RightConnect. Check their profile details and send a request to a suitable provider. Visit rightconnect.app.

**Edit:** 0-3s hook; 3-10s search/profile footage; 10-15s CTA. Read naturally; shorten rather than speed up.

## Alternative hooks

1. **General services:** "You know what help you need. Finding whom to ask is the hard part."
2. **Business support:** "Looking for marketing help for your business? Start by exploring the people behind the service."
3. **Research first:** "Before you contact a provider, get to know what they offer."
4. **Insurance variation, subject to live supply and compliance review:** "Have questions about insurance? Looking for a professional who can explain your options?"

Use only a service category supported by actual providers in the advertised region. RightConnect is not being advertised as an insurer or a source of policy recommendations.
