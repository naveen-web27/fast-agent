# Experts and companies: English video ad

Audience: Independent experts and service companies, in one video.

Goal: Join RightConnect and create an expert or company profile.

Tone: Friendly Indian English. Practical and clear, not a promise of guaranteed customers.

Production/claims rules: [team brief](README.md). Companion customer video: [customers-english.md](customers-english.md#final-version-english-45-55s).

## Final version (English, 45-55s)

Same structure and visuals as the [Tanglish final version](experts-companies-tamil.md#final-version-tanglish-45-55s).

### Continuous voiceover

> Are you an expert, or do you run a company? Here's a good app to show your skills and services to the customers who need them.
>
> Create your expert or company profile on RightConnect, and show your skills, services, and experience in one place.
>
> Digital marketing, billing, tax filing, insurance… customers can look at your skills and services, find you easily, and send you a request.
>
> Look at the requests you receive, accept the ones that fit your work, and start the conversation.
>
> If you're an expert and the customer needs a company's help, invite a company into the same conversation. If you're a company, experts can bring you into customer conversations. Expert and company, working together for the customer in one conversation.
>
> Join today at rightconnect.app and create your profile.

### 30-second cut

> Are you an expert, or do you run a company? RightConnect is a good app to show your skills and services to the customers who need them.
>
> Customers can find you easily through your profile and send you a request. Accept the ones that fit and start the conversation.
>
> Experts and companies can even work together with the customer in one conversation.
>
> Join today at rightconnect.app.
