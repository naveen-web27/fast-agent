Audience: Independent experts and service companies, in one video.

Goal: Join RightConnect and create an expert or company profile.

Tone: Nanbar oru useful website-ai arimugam seiyura maadhiri. Practical delivery; guaranteed customers promise vendaam.

Production/claims rules: [team brief](README.md). Companion customer video: [customers-tamil.md](customers-tamil.md#final-version-tanglish-45-55s).

### Final-version checks

- Do not promise customers, requests, invites, or sales. "Customers ungala thedi kandupidikkalaam" describes the feature, not demand; avoid "customers thedi varuvaanga".
- Only customers start requests. Experts/companies cannot browse customers or send them requests, so do not say or show that.
- Only an expert who has already accepted a request can invite a company. Do not show companies inviting experts, or invites happening automatically.
- Named categories must have live providers in the target region; drop any that don't. Insurance and tax providers must be appropriately authorized; RightConnect does not sell, recommend, or file anything.
- Do not show verification badges, ratings, or track-record numbers unless they are real values on approved demo accounts.
