## Publishing Checklist

- Check each platform's current field limits in its editor; short bios here are conservative, but platform rules can change.
- Use the same available handle everywhere where possible. Do not publish social URLs until the accounts exist.
- Upload your brand logo as the profile picture and check its crop on mobile.
- Use "RightConnect" and "Stop guessing. Start connecting." on covers; keep text clear of avatar overlays and mobile crops.
- Confirm the website, support email, and WhatsApp number before publishing.
- Preview links and make sure visitors can access the deployed website.
- Do not claim every profile is verified, guaranteed outcomes, guaranteed leads, instant replies, or automatic matching.
- Do not publish invented ratings, customer counts, testimonials, or platform verification badges.
- Provider profiles have a free tier; do not describe all provider features as free. Customer accounts are always free.
- Keep prices out of evergreen bios. Publish plan prices or payment announcements only after production checkout is ready and the actual prices are configured.
