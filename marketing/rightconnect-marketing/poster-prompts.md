# RightConnect Marketing: ad poster prompts (to get our own leads)

Paste a prompt into ChatGPT, Gemini or another image tool. Every poster sends people to WhatsApp **+91 63825 95243**, and each new chat is added to the leads page with "Came from: Instagram ad" / "Facebook ad".

## Before you generate

- **Size:** 1080 × 1350 (4:5) for the Instagram/Facebook feed. For Story/Reels/WhatsApp Status, ask for 1080 × 1920 (9:16) with the same prompt.
- **Brand colours:** navy `#101b31`, teal `#2ee6c8`, violet `#8b6cff`, pink `#ff4f8b`, amber `#ffb547`.
- **Text:** AI tools often misspell words, especially Tamil. If the text comes out wrong, add "**no text at all**" to the prompt and type the text in Canva.
- **Price:** the Starter pack is **₹599** (3 posters). Change the badge if your offer changes.
- **No fake claims:** don't add "1000+ clients", ratings or results we can't prove.

## How to run the ad

1. In Meta Ads Manager choose the **Engagement → Messaging (WhatsApp)** goal so the button opens a chat.
2. Target your city and nearby areas, ages 24–55, and interests like small business, entrepreneurship and shop owners.
3. Run 2–3 posters with a small daily budget for 3–4 days, keep the one that brings the most WhatsApp chats, and pause the rest.
4. Add every new chat on the leads page the same day and set a follow-up date.
